# ScholarSphere

India's Scholarship Discovery Platform.

ScholarSphere helps students discover scholarships offered by government organisations, companies, universities, NGOs, and foundations across India.

## What the platform does

- Discover scholarships by education level, course, state, provider, and eligibility.
- View scholarship benefits, eligibility criteria, required documents, and deadlines.
- Check the official source and visit the provider's website to apply.
- Find opportunities that may match a student's academic and personal criteria.

## Important

ScholarSphere is an information and discovery platform. Applications are completed on the scholarship provider's official website. ScholarSphere does not collect application documents or submit applications on behalf of students.

Scholarship details and deadlines must be verified against official provider sources before being presented as current.
